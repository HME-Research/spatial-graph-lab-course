Page 2 of 12 · A. Use the app

Use the app in your browser

Nothing to install. Open spatial-graph-lab.vercel.app in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop or desktop.

01

Start a project

Choose how to start, then find your way around.

  1. Choose how to startThe Welcome screen offers Create project (an empty graph, with the toolkit chosen beside it), Open project (a project file from your computer) and Open example… (the example house, or a toolkit's designs).
  2. Learn the panelsThe Guide button (in the app and on the Welcome screen) explains graphs, attributes, every view and panel, and has step-by-step How-to entries with screenshots.
02

Design, analyse and transform

The graph drives the building: change one, see the other.

  1. DesignAdd nodes, connect them with Access or Open edges, give them Functions and attributes, and watch the building update in every view.
  2. Analyse and transformAnalyse reads your design and shows an answer; Transform proposes a change: preview it, Apply, and one Undo reverses it.

Want your own? Your own analysis and Your own transform show how, with an AI chat.

03

Keep your work

Save in this browser, and export a file you can keep.

Your projects are kept in this browser on this computer only. Another computer, another browser, a private window or clearing your browsing data will not have them. Export a file to keep or hand in your work.

  1. Save in this browserOpen Project (left rail) › Save locally. Your last session also comes back by itself: the Welcome screen lists your recent projects.
  2. Export a fileProject › Export project downloads one .project.json file with every scenario and every analysis and transform you uploaded.
  3. Open it againOn any computer: Welcome › Open project › choose the file. It opens as a separate copy.

Windows

The exported file lands in your Downloads folder: open File Explorer › Downloads. Move it somewhere safe, such as Documents or a cloud folder.

macOS

The exported file lands in your Downloads folder: open Finder › Downloads. Move it somewhere safe, such as Documents or a cloud folder.

Troubleshooting — common issues
My project is gone
  1. Check you are in the same browser on the same computer, not in a private window.
  2. Look in the Welcome screen's list of recent projects.
  3. Otherwise, open your last exported .project.json with Open project. Export after every session so this never costs you work.
I can't find the exported file
  1. Look in your Downloads folder (see the tabs above).
  2. Your browser's downloads list (Ctrl+J in Chrome and EdgeCmd+Option+L in Chrome and Safari) shows where it went.
04

Hand in a project

One file holds your design and everything you uploaded.

Export the project (step 03) and hand in the .project.json file the way your tutor asks (for example on Moodle). It contains your design, its scenarios and the analyses and transforms you uploaded.

05

Toolkits online

Which toolkits the online app has.

The online app has the course's toolkits (such as the House toolkit). Your own toolkit appears there only once the course publishes it; until then, run it on your computer (Set up), and your tutor can open your pushed branch online.
