Page 2 of 12 · A. Use the app

Use the app in your browser

Nothing to install: open spatial-graph-lab.vercel.app in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop. This page is for learning the app; your course work is your toolkit (pages 5–11).

01

Start a project

Choose how to start, then find your way around.
  1. Choose how to startThe Welcome screen offers Create project (an empty graph), Open project (a project file from your computer) and Open example… (the example house, or a toolkit's designs). The Toolkit picker under them chooses the Functions a new project starts with.
  2. Learn the panelsThe Guide button explains graphs, attributes and every view and panel; its How to… tab has step-by-step entries with pictures.
02

Design, analyse and transform

The graph drives the building: change one, see the other.
  1. DesignAdd nodes, connect them with Access or Open edges, give them Functions and attributes, and watch the building update in every view.
  2. Analyse and transformAnalyse reads your design and shows an answer. Transform proposes a change: you see a preview, press Apply, and one Undo reverses it.

Want your own? Your own analysis and Your own transform show how, with an AI chat.

03

Keep your work

Save in this browser, and export a file you can keep.

Export after every session. Your projects live only in this browser on this computer. Another computer, another browser, a private window or clearing your browsing data will not have them. Project › Export project gives you a file that does.

  1. Save in this browserProject (left rail) › Save locally. The Welcome screen lists your recent projects.
  2. Export a fileProject › Export project downloads one .project.json file with every scenario and every analysis and transform you uploaded.
  3. Open it againOn any computer: Welcome › Open project › choose the file. It opens as a separate copy.

The file lands in your Downloads folder (File Explorer › DownloadsFinder › Downloads). Move it somewhere safe, such as Documents or a cloud folder.

Lost a project? Help › My project is gone.

04

Toolkits in the app

The online app has the course's toolkits; yours runs on your laptop.

The online app has the course's toolkits, such as House. Your own toolkit runs on your laptop (Set up); once you push it, your tutor opens it on their computer, and online once previews are set up.

No laptop that can run the app? Tell your tutor early. Only if your tutor agrees, you may hand in browser work instead: Export project and send the .project.json file as your tutor asks.
